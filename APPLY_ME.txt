MEOW ISLAND v6 (adds the Step goals card widget). Apply on top of v5. Unzip INTO your project folder (e.g. C:\si).
One command per line in PowerShell. If your browser saved it as 'meow-island-v6 (1).zip', use that name.

  cd C:\si
  Expand-Archive -Path "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\meow-island-v6.zip" -DestinationPath C:\si -Force

CHECK (each must print one line; if one prints nothing the old file is still there):

  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\StepCardRenderer.kt -Pattern "object StepCardRenderer"
  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\MeowWidgets.kt -Pattern "class MeowStepCardWidget"
  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\IslandModule.kt -Pattern "MeowStepCardWidget"
  Select-String -Path plugins\withIsland.js -Pattern "meow_widget_stepcard_info"
  Get-ChildItem plugins\widgets\res -Recurse -File | Measure-Object | Select-Object Count      # expect 20

Then (prompt must say PS C:\si>):

  npx expo prebuild --platform android --clean
  cd android
  .\gradlew.bat assembleRelease -PreactNativeArchitectures=arm64-v8a

After installing: Settings tab > Home screen widgets > Step goals card (or long-press home > Widgets > Meow Island > Meow Step Goals).
Turn on Health > Step counter / Daily step goal / Contribution heatmap to fill it. The other widgets are unchanged.
