MEOW ISLAND v7: reliable background step counting + Dancing Cat music widget. Apply on top of v6.
Unzip INTO your project folder (e.g. C:\si). One command per line in PowerShell.
If your browser saved it as 'meow-island-v7 (1).zip', use that name.

  cd C:\si
  Expand-Archive -Path "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\meow-island-v7.zip" -DestinationPath C:\si -Force

CHECK (each must print one line; if one prints nothing the old file is still there):

  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\StepService.kt -Pattern "class StepService"
  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\HealthEngine.kt -Pattern "liveLatencyUs"
  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\MeowMusicWidget.kt -Pattern "object MeowMusic"
  Select-String -Path plugins\kotlin\IslandModule.kt -Pattern "to 7"
  Select-String -Path plugins\withIsland.js -Pattern "meow_widget_music_info"
  Get-ChildItem plugins\widgets\res -Recurse -File | Measure-Object | Select-Object Count      # expect 45

Then (prompt must say PS C:\si>):

  npx expo prebuild --platform android --clean
  cd android
  .\gradlew.bat assembleRelease -PreactNativeArchitectures=arm64-v8a

After installing:
 * Health tab: switch on a step feature (e.g. Step counter), grant Physical activity, keep "Count steps in the background" on.
   You should see a silent notification "Counting your steps". Lock the phone, walk ~30 steps, unlock: the total and the widgets refresh.
 * Settings tab > Home screen widgets > Dancing cat music (or long-press home > Widgets > Meow Island > Meow Dancing Cat).
   Play music: the cat dances; pause: it stops. Settings > Features > Dancing Cat Mode turns the dancing off.
 * Music widget needs Notification access (same one the island uses) to SHOW the track; buttons still work without it.
